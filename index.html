A GUIDE MADE FOR EXPLORING

How to use
Good Days.

A simple way to discover more of what's happening around Crows Nest.

01

Explore

Browse six interest areas or use the search and location filters. Each activity includes an organiser link and practical details.

02

Make a shortlist

Mark an activity as “Interested”, “Maybe later” or “Not for me”. You can also add private notes or questions to ask.

03

Follow up

Open the organiser's website to check dates, vacancies and access. Share or print your shortlist to arrange an outing with someone.

GOOD TO KNOW

Comfort and accessibility first.

Activities were selected with shorter outings, seated participation and shoulder recovery in mind. Some music and art activities will need to wait until a treating clinician confirms it is safe to resume them.

Before attending, confirm whether there are steps or ramps, how far it is from drop-off to the activity room, seating options, accessible toilets and the length of the session.
